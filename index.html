      THE IDEA BEHIND THE SEARCH

      How A* finds its way.

      f(n) = g(n) + h(n)
      g

      Cost so far

      The number of legal blank-tile moves from the initial board to the current state. Each move costs one.

      h

      Estimated distance

      Manhattan sums tile distances; Misplaced counts tiles outside their goal cells. Both ignore the blank.

      f

      Best candidate

      A* expands the state with the lowest combined cost. These admissible heuristics preserve optimality for unit-cost moves.

      Why it works

      With a finite search space, non-negative move costs, an admissible heuristic, and correct best-path handling, A* is complete and returns an optimal solution. Its main cost is memory: a difficult board can require a large frontier.
